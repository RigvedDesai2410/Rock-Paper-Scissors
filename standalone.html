SUPER ROCK PAPER SCISSORS

A retro 16-bit arcade simulation of the classic Rock-Paper-Scissors game featuring authentic Mario-style pixelated human hands and perspective depth animation!

HOW TO PLAY:

  • Select ✊ ROCK, 🖐️ PAPER, or ✌️ SCISSORS using the buttons or keyboard.
  • Watch the authentic 3-beat rhythm shake: "Rock! Paper! Scissors! SHOOT!"
  • Hands thrust forward in 3D perspective into the center clash zone!

RULES:

  • ✊ Rock crushes ✌️ Scissors
  • 🖐️ Paper covers ✊ Rock
  • ✌️ Scissors cut 🖐️ Paper

KEYBOARD SHORTCUTS:

  • 1 or R: Throw Rock
  • 2 or P: Throw Paper
  • 3 or S: Throw Scissors
  • M: Toggle Mute / Sound

SCORING SYSTEM:

Only your Wins, Draws, and Streaks are recorded. Regroup and conquer your high score!
